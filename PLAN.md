# CS802 — Internship II (Optional) — PLAN

**Semester:** 8 · **Credits:** 4 · **Level:** Professional
**Theory:** 0 hrs · **Internship:** 8–12 weeks (concurrent with CS801 if possible) · **Tools:** Whatever the host organisation uses

## Course Format
An **optional** second internship, typically taken concurrently with the final thesis if the student has an offer from a company that supports part-time work, or as a full-time placement during a gap semester arrangement. Students who do not take CS802 fulfil the requirement with extra credits in CS801 or CS803.

## Objectives

1. Take on a role with more responsibility than CS705 — a real owner, not just a contributor.
2. Deepen the understanding of a specific industry domain (fintech, health, AI infra, etc.).
3. Build a network of professional relationships that continues after the internship.
4. Produce work that could credibly be published or open-sourced.

## Internship Requirements

### Before the Internship
1. **Host secured** — offer letter or confirmed engagement
2. **Role defined** — what the student will own end to end
3. **Two supervisors** — host supervisor and academic supervisor
4. **Plan agreed** — 1-page plan with deliverables
5. **Overlap check** — if concurrent with CS801, schedule and workload approved by the academic supervisor

### During the Internship (Weekly)
1. **Weekly log** — 200 words: done, blocked, learned, next
2. **Bi-weekly 1:1** — scheduled check-in with host supervisor
3. **Monthly check-in** — with academic supervisor
4. **Contribution record** — list of commits, PRs, docs, or decisions

### After the Internship
1. **Final report** — 2,500 words (structure below)
2. **Deliverable showcase** — what was actually shipped
3. **Exit presentation** — 15 minutes to academic supervisor + one other faculty member
4. **Reference letter** — requested from the host supervisor

## Final Report Structure
Host organisation, team, and my role

The problem I owned — its business or technical context

Approach — how I decomposed and solved it

Deliverables — what actually shipped

Impact — measured or estimated effect on the organisation

Challenges — technical, political, personal

Reflection — what I would do differently next time

Reference letter (attached)

text

## Weekly Log Structure

Each week's `weekly/week-NN.md`:
- **Shipped** — what shipped this week (link or description)
- **Blocked** — what is stuck, who is helping, escalation
- **Learned** — one concrete technical or organisational learning
- **Next** — plan for the coming week

## Assessment

| Component | Weight |
| :--- | :---: |
| Host supervisor evaluation | 35% |
| Scope and impact of work (vs a typical intern role) | 25% |
| Weekly logs and engagement | 15% |
| Final report (rigour, honesty, clarity) | 15% |
| Exit presentation | 10% |

## If the Internship Is Not Taken

The 4 credits are transferred to:
- Extra project scope in CS801 (approved by supervisor), **or**
- An additional elective from CS803 with an extended project component

The alternative must be approved by the academic supervisor before the semester starts.

## Failure Modes to Avoid

- Treating the internship as a fresh start — this is the second internship, expectations are higher.
- Overlapping poorly with CS801 — if both are concurrent, budget time honestly.
- Under-reporting blockers — escalate early, not in the final report.
- Claiming impact that cannot be evidenced — numbers or links only.
